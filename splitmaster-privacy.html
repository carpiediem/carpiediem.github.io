SplitMaster Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

SplitMaster ("the app") is a stopwatch app for timing swim meets, developed by Ryan Carpenter. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information stored on your device

Heat, lane, and split data you record — event/heat numbers, lap times, and history — is stored locally on your device only. It is not sent to us or to any server we operate; we don't run a backend for the app at all.

Google Account access (optional)

If you choose to export a heat to Google Sheets (a SplitMaster Pro feature), the app asks you to sign in with Google and requests two permissions:

  • See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (drive.file scope) — used solely to create the spreadsheet SplitMaster exports to. The app cannot see or modify any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address — used only to show which Google account is currently connected in Settings.

Your Google sign-in tokens are stored securely on your device (using the platform Keychain/Keystore) and are never sent to us. You can disconnect your Google account at any time from Settings, and you can revoke SplitMaster's access entirely from your Google Account permissions page.

SplitMaster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In-app purchases

SplitMaster Pro is sold as an in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iOS). Payment details and purchase records are handled by Google/Apple under their own privacy policies — SplitMaster only receives confirmation of whether you own the purchase, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage-tracking SDKs
  • No crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties
  • No account system of our own — there's nothing for us to store

Children's privacy

SplitMaster is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.
