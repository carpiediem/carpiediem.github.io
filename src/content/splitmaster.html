Splitmaster is a mobile stopwatch app for timing swim or track meets. It tracks per-lane splits across multiple lanes in a heat, lets you edit or delete a recorded split mid-heat, and saves completed heats to a history you can export later. I built it after years of hand-timing and volunteer swim officiating left me wanting something purpose-built for tracking splits across a whole heat at once, not just a single stopwatch.

It's built with Expo and Expo Router, running on iOS, Android, and web from a single TypeScript codebase, with React Native Paper for the UI. It's currently in testing via the Play Store's internal testing track.
