My kids got into Rubik's Cubes, and the usual tutorials I found online moved too fast or buried the useful bits in a wall of video. So I put together a slide deck that walks through a beginner's layer-by-layer method one step at a time, with a picture of each move so they could pause on whichever step was giving them trouble.

It's been handy as a reference to point them back to whenever they get stuck mid-solve, rather than re-explaining the same algorithm from scratch every time.
